About me
======
I am a PhD student working at the Cosmic Dawn Center, as part of the Niels Bohr Institute, University of Copenhagen. 

I am interested in the earliest galaxy formation, and mostly work with James Webb Space Telescope spectroscopic observations of high-redshift galaxies to study their gas properties and chemical abundance patterns.
